THE RULES ARE SIMPLE

How to play SOS.

A little strategy goes a long way. Here’s everything you need to know.

01

The goal

Make as many S–O–S patterns as you can. A pattern can run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally—in either direction.

SOSHORIZONTALSOSDIAGONAL
02

Take your turn

Choose either S or O, then place it in any empty square. Players take turns; in Solo, the computer plays too.

✦ You choose the letter every turn—use that to set up a pattern or block one.
03

Score & keep going

Each new SOS you complete earns one point. If your move makes multiple patterns, you score for each one—and you get another turn.

SOS→+1 pointONE NEW COMBINATION
04

Game over

When every square is filled, the player with the most SOS patterns wins. A tied score means it’s a draw. Good luck!

✦Pro tip

Before you place a letter, check all eight directions. One move can complete more than one SOS at a time.
